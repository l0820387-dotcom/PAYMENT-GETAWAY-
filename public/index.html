How it works

Three steps, no dashboard required

Everything happens through the API and a Telegram bot — generate keys, watch verifications, manage your account, all from chat.

01

Generate a QR or collect a UTR

Call GET /api/qr with a UPI ID and optional amount to get a payment QR and an order_id — or just collect the UTR your customer already received from their bank.

02

Submit for verification

Call POST /api/verify with the amount and UTR. You get back a txn_id immediately with a pending status.

03

Get notified when it clears

We match the UTR against your connected inbox's incoming FamPay emails. Poll GET /api/verify/status/:txn_id or set a webhook and get pushed the result.

API reference

Four endpoints, that's the whole surface

GET/api/qr
Generate a UPI payment QR + order_id. Params: upi, amount (optional).
POST/api/verify
Submit amount + UTR for verification. Returns 202 pending immediately.
GET/api/verify/status/:txn_id
Poll for the current status of a submitted verification.
GET/api/history
Fetch your own payment history, filterable by status.
Under the hood

Built so your Gmail credentials never touch plaintext

🔐

Encrypted at rest

Gmail App Passwords are encrypted with AES-256-GCM before storage. The raw password is never written to disk.

#

Hashed API keys

Your key is shown once at generation. We only ever store its SHA-256 hash afterward.

⧉

No duplicate UTRs

Each UTR can only be submitted once across the whole system — replay attempts are rejected outright.

◎

IP whitelisting

Optionally lock your key to specific server IPs via a single bot command.

↻

Rate limited

Per-key limits with instant Telegram alerts the moment you cross them.

▤

Full history

Every verification — success, failed, expired — logged and queryable by status.
